Physical Slope Extraction

AETHON ingests a 1,500 × 1,500 pixel floating-point GeoTIFF derived from NASA HiRISE stereo pairs, computing real slope in degrees via central difference gradients.

  • Gentle Traverse (<10°): Low-effort corridor (91.4% of study site)
  • Moderate Grade (10–15°): Climbing grade requiring pacing (7.8%)
  • Steep Grade (15–20°): Approaching trafficability limit (0.8%)
  • Impassable Scarp (≥20°): High-angle barrier cutoff

SOLAR ILLUMINATION & TERRAIN RADIATION SHIELDING SLIDE 6 OF 7

Solar Ephemeris, Cliff Shadows & Terrain Radiation Shielding

Predicting diurnal solar lighting and evaluating two-tier natural radiation shelter potential.

Solar Ephemeris & Cliff Shadows

  • NASA Mars24 Ephemeris: Computes solar altitude and azimuth from Allison & McEwen (2000) equations across diurnal cycle (Sol 4452 Ls = 84.3°).
  • Terrain Solar Ray-Tracing: Evaluates direct sunlight vs cliff shadows with Mars curvature drop (R_M = 3,389.5 km) and ±0.25m vertical precision.
  • 4-State Visibility: Classifies target line-of-sight into VISIBLE, OCCLUDED, UNCERTAIN AT DTM RESOLUTION (grazing within ±0.25m), or INSUFFICIENT COVERAGE.
  • Crucial Scientific Distinction: Optical shadow blocks direct solar photons for lighting/thermal context; it does NOT provide omnidirectional GCR radiation shielding.

Terrain Radiation Shielding (Tiers 1 & 2)

  • Tier 1 Geometry-Only Shelter Potential: 360° horizon ray-march (72 sectors) computes blocked sky solid angle (Ω_blocked) and fraction (F_blocked). Prominently labeled: "Geometry only; not a dose estimate."
  • Empirical Murray Buttes Validation: MSL RAD observed Butte M12 (Sols 1456–1467): 19% sky view blocked produced 4.0% ± 1.2% dose reduction and 7.5% neutral-particle reduction (Ehresmann et al. 2021).
  • Tier 2 Transport Benchmark: GEANT4 downward zenith model (Khaksari et al. 2022) with secondary albedo neutrons in rock mass (η ≈ 0.80), reproducing M12 benchmark.
  • Research Prototype Scope: Planning aid only; never label a route or shelter "safe."

METHODOLOGY & READINESS SLIDE 7 OF 7

Methodology, Scientific Scope & Software Quality

Rigorous, honest engineering that bridges planetary data archives with exploration planning.
Research Prototype Scope

AETHON is a research planning tool for candidate traverse evaluation and line-of-sight estimation. Outputs are planning aids, not flight-ready EVA safety advice or human life-support guidance. Never label a route or shelter "safe."

Scientific Integrity & Sourced Constraints

CRISM spectral units provide regional stratigraphic context; REMS readings are dated point observations; no accessible water exists at this equatorial site; Jezero electrostatics are not extrapolated to Gale.

Two-Tier Validation Hierarchy (43 Unit Tests + Scientific Manifest)

Tier 1: 43 automated unit tests across 5 QA modules (DTM bounds, A* graph search, 4-state viewshed, Mars24 solar shadows, and radiation shelter integration). Tier 2 Scientific Manifest verifies photogrammetric precision and M12 validation.

Offline Zero-Dependency Delivery

Runs cleanly in any standard web browser via Python's built-in HTTP server without external CDNs, npm builds, or internet access required.
